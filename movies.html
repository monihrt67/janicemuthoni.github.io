my favourites

1. RYE LANE (2023)

About: Two twenty-somethings, both reeling from bad break-ups, connect over the course of an eventful day in South London helping each other deal with their nightmare exes, and potentially restoring their faith in romance.

Why I find it good: the camera work and the authentic portrayal of young love.

2. MAMMA MIA (2008)

About: A mother. A daughter. Three possible fathers. A trip down the aislE youll never forget.

Why I find it good: the catchy soundtrack and the feel-good atmosphere.

3. BIG HERO 6 (2014)

About: A young boy and his robot companion navigate the challenges of high school while trying to solve a mystery.

Why I find it good: the heartwarming story and the impressive animation.

4. BEAUTIFUL BOY (2018)

About: A young boy's journey through the challenges of growing up in a difficult environment.

Why I find it good: the powerful performances and the authentic depiction of childhood struggles.

5. PROJECT HAIL MARY (2026)

About: A lone astronaut must save the world from an alien threat.

Why I find it good: the engaging storyline and the impressive visual effects.
